A short formal message — to a school, a **hyresvärd** (landlord) or an authority — follows a simple shape: a greeting, a short reason for writing, and a polite closing. You open with **Hej,** and go straight to the point: **jag skriver angående** (I'm writing regarding) or **gällande** (regarding) the matter.

```example
Hej, jag skriver angående min sons frånvaro igår. — Hello, I'm writing regarding my son's absence yesterday.
Gällande fakturan vill jag fråga om förfallodagen. — Regarding the invoice, I'd like to ask about the due date.
```

If you attach something, say so: **jag bifogar** (I'm attaching) a certificate or a document, and give the message a clear **ämne** (subject) if it's an email. State clearly what you want to happen next.

```example
Jag bifogar ett läkarintyg till detta mejl. — I'm attaching a doctor's certificate to this email.
Jag vore tacksam om ni kunde återkomma innan fredag. — I would be grateful if you could get back to me before Friday.
```

Close politely with **Med vänlig hälsning** (Kind regards) and your name. A short, clear message like this is often all that's needed — there's no need to over-explain.
