When your child has **feber** (a fever) or is **smittsam** (contagious), they stay home from preschool — and often so do you. This is **VAB** (temporary parental benefit); you say you **vabba** (take VAB leave) for the day.

```example
Jag vabbar idag för att sonen har hög feber. — I'm on VAB today because my son has a high fever.
Vattkoppor är smittsamt, så hon stannar hemma. — Chickenpox is contagious, so she's staying home.
```

You must **anmäla VAB** (report it) — usually the same day, in an app or on **Försäkringskassan**'s website — and separately **meddela förskolan** (notify the preschool) that the child won't be coming.

```example
Anmäl VAB i appen samma dag som du är hemma. — Report VAB in the app the same day you're at home.
Meddela förskolan innan klockan åtta om barnet är sjukt. — Notify the preschool before eight o'clock if the child is ill.
```

If unsure how serious the **symtom** (symptoms) are — a fever, or a child who **kräks** (vomits) — you can call **1177** for advice. As a rule, a child should be **frisk** (well) for a full day before going back.
