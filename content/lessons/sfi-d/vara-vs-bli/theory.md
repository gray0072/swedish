**Vara** (to be) describes a state as it already is; **bli** (to become) describes the change into that state. The choice is simple once you ask: is this a snapshot, or a transition?

```example
Hon är trött idag. — She's tired today. (state)
Hon blev trött efter jobbet. — She got tired after work. (change into that state)
```

The same pair works for weather, profession, or mood — anywhere something shifts from one state to another.

```example
Det blir mörkt tidigt på vintern. — It gets dark early in winter.
Han blev sjuk i helgen, men han är frisk igen nu. — He got ill over the weekend, but he's well again now.
```

**Bli** also names becoming something over time, like a profession:

```example
Hon är lärare. Hon blev lärare 2015. — She's a teacher. She became a teacher in 2015.
```

A quick test: if you can insert "suddenly" or "gradually" and it still makes sense, you likely need **bli**; if you're just stating a fact as it stands, use **vara**.
