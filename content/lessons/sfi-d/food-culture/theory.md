**Husmanskost** — traditional Swedish home cooking — includes dishes like **köttbullar** (meatballs) with potatoes and lingonberry jam, often described as **lagom**: not too much of anything, just the right amount.

```example
Köttbullar med potatis är klassisk husmanskost. — Meatballs with potatoes are classic husmanskost.
Det är lagom kryddat, precis som jag gillar det. — It's spiced just right, exactly how I like it.
```

**Fika** almost always involves something sweet, like a **kanelbulle** (cinnamon bun), or a simple **smörgås** (open sandwich) at other times of day.

```example
En kanelbulle passar perfekt till fikat. — A cinnamon bun goes perfectly with fika.
Han äter en smörgås med ost till frukost. — He eats an open cheese sandwich for breakfast.
```

Alcohol above a certain strength can only be bought at **Systembolaget**, the state-owned monopoly shop — with its own limited **öppettider** (opening hours, usually closed on Sundays) and a strict **18-årsgräns** (18-year age limit): always bring **legitimation** (ID). Regular grocery stores **stänga** (close) at set times too, often earlier on Sundays.
