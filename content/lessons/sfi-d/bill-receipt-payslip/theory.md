A **faktura** (bill/invoice) always carries the same key numbers: the **belopp** (amount) to pay, the **förfallodag** (due date), and an **OCR-nummer** — a reference number you must enter correctly so the payment is matched to the right bill.

```example
Fakturan ska betalas senast på förfallodagen. — The bill must be paid by the due date at the latest.
Ange OCR-numret när du betalar fakturan. — Enter the OCR number when you pay the bill.
```

A shop **kvitto** (receipt) is worth keeping — it is your proof of purchase if you want to return or exchange something, and it shows how much **moms** (VAT) was included in the price.

```example
Spara kvittot om du vill byta varan senare. — Keep the receipt if you want to exchange the item later.
Priset på kvittot inkluderar moms. — The price on the receipt includes VAT.
```

A **lönespecifikation** (payslip) looks more complicated, but the outline is simple: it shows your **bruttolön** (gross pay, before tax), the **skatteavdrag** (tax deduction), and finally your **nettolön** (net pay) — the amount actually paid into your account. Many regular bills, like rent or the phone subscription, can be set up on **autogiro** (direct debit) so you don't have to **betala en faktura** (pay a bill) manually every month.
