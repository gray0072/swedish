Most adjectives just add -t (ett-words) or -a (plural/definite), but **liten** ("little/small") is irregular and has four distinct forms you need to memorise separately.

```example
En liten hund sprang förbi. — A little dog ran past. (en-word, indefinite)
Ett litet hus står på kullen. — A little house stands on the hill. (ett-word, indefinite)
```

In the **definite** singular, both genders take **lilla** instead of the expected weak ending:

```example
Den lilla hunden heter Bruno. — The little dog is called Bruno.
Det lilla huset på kullen är mitt. — The little house on the hill is mine.
```

In the **plural**, both genders take **små** — a completely different word from *liten*, not just an ending change.

```example
Små barn leker i parken. — Little children are playing in the park.
De små husen ligger nära varandra. — The little houses are close to each other.
```

Summary table:

| form | context | example |
|---|---|---|
| liten | en-word, indefinite | en liten hund |
| litet | ett-word, indefinite | ett litet hus |
| lilla | definite, singular, either gender | den lilla hunden |
| små | plural, either gender | små hundar |
