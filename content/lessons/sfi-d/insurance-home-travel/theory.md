A **hemförsäkring** (home insurance) is something almost everyone in Sweden has — it's often required by the landlord, and it **gäller** (is valid, applies) for far more than fire: **stöld** (theft), a **vattenskada** (water damage) or things broken while moving.

```example
Hemförsäkringen täcker en vattenskada i badrummet. — Home insurance covers water damage in the bathroom.
Har du kollat om försäkringen gäller vid stöld? — Have you checked whether the insurance covers theft?
```

A **reseförsäkring** (travel insurance) does the same job abroad — often it's already included in your hemförsäkring, or comes with certain credit cards, so check before buying a separate one.

```example
Reseförsäkringen gäller när jag är utomlands. — Travel insurance is valid when I'm abroad.
```

If something happens, you **anmäla en skada** (report a claim) to your **försäkringsbolag** (insurance company), usually online or in an app. You then pay a **självrisk** (deductible) — a fixed amount you cover yourself — before the rest comes as **ersättning** (compensation).

```example
Jag anmälde skadan i appen samma dag. — I reported the claim in the app the same day.
Självrisken var 1500 kronor, resten fick jag som ersättning. — The deductible was 1,500 kronor, the rest I got as compensation.
```
