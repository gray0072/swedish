Many Swedes **träna** (train, exercise) regularly, whether at a **gym** or by joining a **förening** — a club, often run largely by volunteers, for football, swimming or another sport. A yearly **medlemsavgift** (membership fee) is common.

```example
Jag tränar på gymmet tre gånger i veckan för att hålla mig i form. — I work out at the gym three times a week to keep fit.
Min son spelar fotboll i en förening, och medlemsavgiften betalas en gång om året. — My son plays football in a club, and the membership fee is paid once a year.
```

If your child trains in a förening, you'll often need to **skjutsa** (drive/give a lift) them there and back, remember their **träningskläder** (sports clothes), and sometimes watch a **match** from the sideline.

```example
Jag skjutsar barnen till träningen på onsdagar. — I drive the kids to training on Wednesdays.
Matchen börjar klockan fem, glöm inte träningskläderna. — The match starts at five, don't forget the sports clothes.
```

At the gym or the club, everyone changes in the **omklädningsrum** (changing room), and a **tränare** (coach) usually leads the session.
