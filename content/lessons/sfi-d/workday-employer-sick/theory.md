Your **schema** (schedule) sets your **arbetstid** (working hours) for the week, along with your **rast** (break) — usually around lunchtime — and any **övertid** (overtime) you agree to. Some workplaces offer **flextid** (flexitime), letting you shift your hours a little.

```example
Mitt schema visar arbetstid och rast för varje dag. — My schedule shows working hours and break for each day.
Vi har flextid, så jag kan börja klockan åtta eller nio. — We have flexitime, so I can start at eight or nine.
```

If you're too ill to work, you **sjukanmäla dig** (call in sick) — normally by calling or messaging your **närmaste chef** (direct manager) as early as possible, so a **kollega** (colleague) can be asked to cover. This is your **sjukanmälan** (sick notification).

```example
Jag sjukanmälde mig till min chef klockan sju. — I called in sick to my manager at seven o'clock.
Kan du be en kollega täcka upp idag? — Can you ask a colleague to cover today?
```

The first sick day usually comes with a **karensavdrag** (a small pay deduction for the waiting period); after about a week off, your employer may ask for a **sjukintyg** (medical certificate) from a doctor.
