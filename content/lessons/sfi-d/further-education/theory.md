After SFI, the next step is often **komvux** (municipal adult education), which offers everything from **SVA grund** (basic Swedish as a second language, split into four **delkurser** — sub-courses) to a **yrkesutbildning** (vocational training) leading straight to a trade.

```example
Efter SFI kurs D kan man börja på SVA grund. — After SFI kurs D you can start SVA grund.
SVA grund har fyra delkurser, en efter en. — SVA grund has four sub-courses, one after another.
```

Finishing a course can give you **behörighet** (eligibility) for the next step, whether that's upper-secondary school or a specific programme. To find the right path, you can book time with a **studie- och yrkesvägledare** (study and careers counsellor).

```example
Den här kursen ger behörighet till gymnasiet. — This course gives you eligibility for upper-secondary school.
Jag bokade tid hos studievägledaren för att fråga om mina alternativ. — I booked an appointment with the study counsellor to ask about my options.
```

Studying can be funded through **CSN**, the agency handling **studiestöd** (study aid): part **studiebidrag** (a grant you never repay) and part **studielån** (a loan you repay once you've finished studying).
