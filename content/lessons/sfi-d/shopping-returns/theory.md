Many Swedish shops offer **öppet köp** (the right to return an item, no reason needed) for a set period, often 30 days — so you can **lämna tillbaka** (return) or **byta varan** (exchange it) simply because you changed your mind. Always **spara kvittot** (keep the receipt) as proof.

```example
Det är öppet köp i 30 dagar, så du kan lämna tillbaka jackan om den inte passar. — There's a 30-day right of return, so you can return the jacket if it doesn't fit.
```

If the item is actually broken, that's different: you **reklamera** (make a complaint) about a **defekt** (fault), which the seller must handle even without öppet köp, often within a **garanti** (warranty) period.

```example
Jag reklamerade skorna eftersom sömmen gick upp direkt. — I complained about the shoes because the seam came apart right away.
Butiken godkände min reklamation och gav mig full återbetalning. — The shop accepted my complaint and gave me a full refund.
```

If a shop refuses a fair **reklamation**, you can turn to **ARN** — the consumer disputes board — which gives a free, non-binding recommendation in the customer's favour when the case is clear.
