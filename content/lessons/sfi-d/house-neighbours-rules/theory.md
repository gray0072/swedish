Living in a Swedish apartment building means sharing a few facilities under written **ordningsregler** (house rules), usually set by the **bostadsrättsförening** (housing cooperative, BRF) or landlord. The **tvättstuga** (shared laundry room) works by booking a **tvättid** (a laundry time slot), not by turning up whenever.

```example
Jag bokar tvättstugan och får en tvättid på lördag. — I book the laundry room and get a slot on Saturday.
```

**Sopsortering** (waste sorting) matters: you **sortera** (sort) plastic, paper, glass and food waste separately, usually in an **återvinningsrum** (recycling room) in the basement or yard.

```example
Vi sorterar plast, papper och glas i återvinningsrummet. — We sort plastic, paper and glass in the recycling room.
```

Other common rules: keep the **trapphus** (stairwell) and other **gemensamma utrymmen** (shared spaces) clear — no bikes or shoes left there — and respect **tystnad** (quiet hours), typically from around ten in the evening.
