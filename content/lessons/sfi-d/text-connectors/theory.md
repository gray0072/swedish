A few small words carry a lot of the logic in a written text, linking one sentence to the next. **Dessutom** (moreover) adds another point in the same direction.

```example
Lägenheten är stor. Dessutom är hyran låg. — The flat is big. Moreover, the rent is low.
```

**Däremot** and **å andra sidan** both mean "on the other hand", introducing a contrast — **däremot** is slightly more compact, often placed right after the subject.

```example
Jag gillar te. Han däremot dricker bara kaffe. — I like tea. He, on the other hand, only drinks coffee.
Jobbet är stressigt. Å andra sidan är lönen bra. — The job is stressful. On the other hand, the pay is good.
```

**Alltså** and **därför** both draw a conclusion, but **alltså** feels more like "so, in other words" (restating a logical result), while **därför** is closer to "that's why" (giving the reason for what follows).

```example
Han är sjuk, alltså kommer han inte idag. — He's ill, so he's not coming today.
Det regnade, därför tog vi paraplyet. — It was raining, that's why we took the umbrella.
```

**Till exempel** (for example) introduces an illustration of a point already made — useful whenever a general statement needs a concrete case.
