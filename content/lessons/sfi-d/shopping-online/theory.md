When you **beställa** (order) something online, you get a **beställning** (order) confirmation, and the **leverans** (delivery) usually takes a few days. Many shops show the **fraktkostnad** (shipping cost) up front — often free above a certain amount — and give you a **spårningsnummer** (tracking number).

```example
Jag beställde en tröja, och fraktkostnaden var gratis över 500 kronor. — I ordered a sweater, and shipping was free above 500 kronor.
Använd spårningsnumret för att se var paketet är. — Use the tracking number to see where the parcel is.
```

Instead of home delivery, a **paket** (parcel) often goes to a nearby **utlämningsställe** (collection point), like a local shop, where you pick it up with your ID.

```example
Hämta paketet på utlämningsstället vid mataffären. — Pick up the parcel at the collection point by the grocery store.
```

If something doesn't fit, you **skicka tillbaka** (send it back) — usually by printing a **returetikett** (return label) and dropping the parcel off. Some shops also let you **betala vid leverans** (pay on delivery) instead of paying in advance.
