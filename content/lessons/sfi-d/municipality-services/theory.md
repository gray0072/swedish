Sweden's public services are split between three levels, and it helps to know who does what. **Kommunen** (the municipality, about 290 of them) **ansvarar för** (is responsible for) local, everyday things: **förskola** (preschool), schools, **äldreomsorg** (elderly care), **avfallshantering** (waste management) and **bygglov** (building permits).

```example
Kommunen ansvarar för förskolan och äldreomsorgen. — The municipality is responsible for preschool and elderly care.
Du söker bygglov hos kommunen. — You apply for a building permit at the municipality.
```

**Regionen** (the region, formerly called landsting) runs healthcare — the **vårdcentral** (health centre) and the hospital — and usually the **kollektivtrafik** (public transport) too. **Staten** (the state) handles what is the same everywhere in the country: pensions, unemployment insurance, the police, Skatteverket and Migrationsverket.

```example
Vårdcentralen tillhör regionen, men skolan tillhör kommunen. — The health centre belongs to the region, but the school belongs to the municipality.
Staten betalar ut pensionen. — The state pays out the pension.
```

If you are unsure who to contact, the **socialtjänsten** (social services) at your kommun is often the right first stop for anything to do with children, housing support or a crisis — they can also point you further, to the region or to a state agency.
