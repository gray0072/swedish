**Skulle** + infinitive does two related jobs. First, it softens a request into **ett artigt sätt** (a polite way) of asking — much gentler than a plain "kan du":

```example
Kan du hjälpa mig? — Can you help me? (direct)
Skulle du kunna hjälpa mig? — Could you help me? (more polite)
Jag skulle vilja boka ett bord för två. — I would like to book a table for two.
```

Second, **skulle** expresses something hypothetical or unreal — what would happen under different, imagined conditions, often paired with **om** (if) + preteritum.

```example
Om jag vore rik skulle jag resa mer. — If I were rich, I would travel more.
Jag skulle gärna komma på festen om jag hade tid. — I would gladly come to the party if I had time.
```

Notice the pattern: **skulle** + infinitive names the imagined result, while the **om**-clause (often with *vore* — "were") sets the unreal condition. The same **skulle** softens both a request and a wish — the two uses share the same distancing effect, putting a little space between you and a blunt statement.
