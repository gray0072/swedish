Three infinitive constructions cover purpose, absence, and means. **För att** (in order to) states a goal; **utan att** (without) states something that did NOT happen alongside the main action; **genom att** (by) states the method.

```example
Jag sparar pengar för att köpa en bil. — I'm saving money in order to buy a car.
Hon gick ut utan att säga något. — She left without saying anything.
Han lärde sig svenska genom att läsa mycket. — He learned Swedish by reading a lot.
```

All three are followed directly by the infinitive — no extra subject, since the subject is the same as in the main clause.

```example
Hon lyckades genom att öva varje dag. — She succeeded by practising every day.
Han gick förbi utan att märka det. — He walked past without noticing it.
```

A close relative is **istället för att** (instead of), which contrasts one action with another that didn't happen.

```example
Han cyklar istället för att köra bil. — He cycles instead of driving.
```
