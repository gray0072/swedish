Swedish has two ways to build the passive. The **-s passive** adds -s to the verb and suits general facts, rules, and processes — signs, instructions, routines.

```example
Butiken öppnas klockan tio och stängs klockan sju. — The shop is opened at ten and closed at seven.
Middagen serveras klockan sju varje kväll. — Dinner is served at seven every evening.
```

The **bli passive** (bli + past participle) suits a single, specific event — something that happens once and changes a state.

```example
Skolan blev renoverad förra sommaren. — The school was renovated last summer.
Hon blev vald till ordförande igår. — She was elected chairperson yesterday.
```

Compare the two directly: **renoveras** describes an ongoing or repeated process ("the school gets renovated every few years"), while **blev renoverad** points at one completed event. If you need to name who did it, add **av** (by) plus the agent.

```example
Boken skrevs av en känd författare. — The book was written by a famous author.
Bilen repareras just nu av en mekaniker. — The car is being repaired right now by a mechanic.
```
