A **jobbannons** (job ad) usually lists **krav** (requirements) that are firm — like a driving licence — and things that are only **meriterande** (an advantage) — like advanced Swedish — plus a short list of **arbetsuppgifter** (job duties).

```example
Ett krav är B-körkort, svenska på hög nivå är meriterande. — A driving licence is a requirement, advanced Swedish is an advantage.
Arbetsuppgifterna är kundservice och enklare administration. — The duties are customer service and simple admin work.
```

When you **söka jobb** (look for work) you send an **ansökan** (application), often together with a **personligt brev** (cover letter) that explains your **erfarenhet** (experience) and your **styrka** (strengths) — briefly and concretely.

```example
Jag söker jobb som undersköterska och skickar in min ansökan idag. — I'm looking for work as a care assistant and I'm sending in my application today.
Min styrka är att jag är noggrann och lätt att samarbeta med. — My strength is that I'm thorough and easy to work with.
```

If invited to interview, you may be asked to **presentera dig** (introduce yourself) in a minute or two — name, background, and why this job, whether it's a temporary post or a **tillsvidareanställning** (permanent position) you're after.
