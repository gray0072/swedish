A short **nyhet** (news item) usually has a **rubrik** (headline) that sums up the **händelse** (event), followed by a few sentences with the key facts: who, what, where. Look for **enligt** (according to) — it tells you whose **källa** (source) the claim comes from.

```example
Enligt polisen var ingen skadad i händelsen. — According to the police, no one was hurt in the event.
Vilken är källan till den här uppgiften? — What's the source of this piece of information?
```

News is often grouped as **inrikes** (domestic) or **utrikes** (foreign), and a **myndighet** (authority, government agency) is a common source for practical warnings — about weather, health or safety.

```example
En myndighet varnar för halka i natt. — An authority is warning about icy roads tonight.
Utrikesnyheterna kommer efter sporten. — The foreign news comes after the sport.
```

A good way to check you've understood a short article is to try to **sammanfatta** (summarise) it in one or two sentences, answering simply: **vad handlar det om?** (what is it about?)
