**Som** is the all-purpose relative word — who, which, that — covering people and things alike, as subject or object.

```example
Mannen som bor bredvid är trevlig. — The man who lives next door is nice.
Boken som jag läser är spännande. — The book that I'm reading is exciting.
```

Two relatives are about place: **där** (where) for a fixed location, and **dit** (where/to which) when there's movement toward it — the same distinction as *hemma* vs *hem*.

```example
Staden där jag bor är liten. — The city where I live is small.
Skolan dit hon går ligger nära. — The school she goes to is nearby.
```

**Vars** (whose) is the relative possessive — a little formal, and increasingly often replaced in speech by "som...sin/hans", but still common in writing.

```example
Kvinnan vars bil stals bor här. — The woman whose car was stolen lives here.
```

All four introduce a subordinate clause and follow BIFF word order inside it, just like other subordinate clauses.
