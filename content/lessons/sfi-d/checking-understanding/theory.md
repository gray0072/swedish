Ordinary conversation needs small tools for checking that both sides understood each other. **Eller hur?** (isn't that right?) is the general tag question, inviting agreement on almost any statement.

```example
Det är kallt idag, eller hur? — It's cold today, isn't it?
```

If you didn't catch something, **va?** is the quick, informal way to ask someone to repeat — more formal is **kan du upprepa?** (can you repeat that?).

```example
Va? Kan du säga det igen? — What? Can you say that again?
Förlåt, kan du upprepa vad du sa? — Sorry, can you repeat what you said?
```

To check you understood the *meaning*, not just the words, use **menar du att…?** (do you mean that…?) or add **så att jag förstår rätt** (so that I understand correctly) to your own request for clarification.

```example
Menar du att vi ska vänta till imorgon? — Do you mean that we should wait until tomorrow?
Kan du förklara igen, så att jag förstår rätt? — Can you explain again, so that I understand correctly?
```

And when the answer confirms your guess, **just det** (exactly) is the natural way to agree.
