You already know the simple genitive: add -s to a name or noun. In a longer noun phrase, the rule stays exactly the same — the **-s** goes on the very last word of the whole phrase, not on the "owner" noun itself.

```example
Min kompis bror jobbar här. — My friend's brother works here.
Min kompis systers bror jobbar här. — My friend's sister's brother works here.
```

This also applies to country and place names before another noun: the -s attaches to the full name.

```example
Sveriges regering sammanträder på tisdagar. — Sweden's government meets on Tuesdays.
Frankrikes huvudstad är Paris. — France's capital is Paris.
```

A chain can go two levels deep without trouble — you just add -s once, at the very end of the whole possessive phrase.

```example
Grannens hunds koppel gick sönder. — The neighbour's dog's lead broke.
Min chefs kontor ligger på andra våningen. — My boss's office is on the second floor.
```

The key habit to build: identify the entire phrase that "owns" the following noun, and put the -s only at its very end.
