Contact with your child's school runs through a few regular channels: a **föräldramöte** (parents' meeting) for the whole class, and once or twice a year a one-on-one **utvecklingssamtal** (development talk) about your own child's progress and **betyg** (grades).

```example
Föräldramötet är på torsdag, och utvecklingssamtalet blir nästa vecka. — The parents' meeting is on Thursday, and the development talk is next week.
```

Day-to-day messages — about **läxa** (homework), a trip, or reporting **frånvaro** (absence) — usually go through a **skolplattform** (school platform/app), and the **klasslärare** (class teacher) is your main contact there.

```example
Anmäl frånvaro på skolplattformen innan klockan åtta. — Report the absence on the school platform before eight o'clock.
Klassläraren skickar läxorna via appen varje vecka. — The class teacher sends the homework via the app every week.
```

If something worries you — for example a sign of **mobbning** (bullying), or a question about **fritids** (after-school care) — you can always **boka tid med läraren** (book time with the teacher) directly rather than waiting for the next scheduled meeting.
