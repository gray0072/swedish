This final lesson ties SFI kurs D together: telling a short, connected story about your life in Sweden **hittills** (so far), and your plans **framöver** (going forward). Start from when you arrived, and note the **utveckling** (progress) **sedan dess** (since then).

```example
Jag flyttade hit för tre år sedan, och sedan dess har jag lärt mig mycket. — I moved here three years ago, and since then I've learned a lot.
Hittills har det gått bra, även om det var en utmaning i början. — So far it's gone well, even though it was a challenge at first.
```

When you **se tillbaka på** (look back on) the whole path from SFI kurs A to now, it's worth naming a real **utmaning** (challenge) as well as the **framsteg** (progress) you've made — both make the story believable.

```example
Den största utmaningen var att förstå snabbt talad svenska. — The biggest challenge was understanding fast spoken Swedish.
Jag har gjort stora framsteg, och jag är tacksam för det. — I've made great progress, and I'm grateful for it.
```

Finish by naming your **målsättning** (goal) and your **nästa steg** (next step) — for most people finishing kurs D, that step is SVA grund.
