Sweden is traditionally divided into 25 historic **landskap** (provinces) — like Skåne or Dalarna — grouped into three larger parts: **Götaland** in the south, **Svealand** in the middle (home to the **huvudstad**, capital, Stockholm), and **Norrland** in the north.

```example
Skåne är ett landskap i södra Sverige, i Götaland. — Skåne is a province in southern Sweden, in Götaland.
Stockholm är Sveriges huvudstad och ligger i Svealand. — Stockholm is Sweden's capital and is located in Svealand.
```

The country **gränsar till** (borders) Norway and Finland, with a long **kust** (coast) facing the Baltic Sea. Norrland has **fjäll** (mountains) and large areas of **glesbygd** (sparsely populated countryside), while the south is more densely settled.

```example
Sverige gränsar till Norge i väster och Finland i öster. — Sweden borders Norway in the west and Finland in the east.
Fjällen i norra Sverige lockar många vandrare. — The mountains in northern Sweden attract many hikers.
```

Sweden has about ten million **invånare** (inhabitants), most living in and around the three biggest cities: Stockholm, Göteborg (Gothenburg) and Malmö.
