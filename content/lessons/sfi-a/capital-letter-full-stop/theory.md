A written **mening** (sentence) follows one simple rule, twice: **stor
bokstav** to start, **punkt** to end.

```example
Jag bor i Malmö. — I live in Malmö.
```

Compare the wrong and the right way of writing the very same sentence:

```example
jag bor i malmö — wrong: no capital letter, no full stop
Jag bor i Malmö. — right: capital letter, full stop
```

A sentence needs both a **subjekt** (who or what) and a **verb** (what
happens) — "Jag bor" already is one, short as it is. A single word is not a
sentence: "Malmö." on its own is not a full sentence, even with a capital
letter and a full stop.

Two other end marks work the same way as the full stop, but change the tone:
**frågetecken** (?) for a question, **utropstecken** (!) for an exclamation.

```example
Var bor du? — Where do you live?
Tack! — Thanks!
Jag bor i Malmö. — I live in Malmö.
```

All three still begin with a capital letter — only the end mark changes,
depending on whether you are asking, exclaiming, or simply stating something.

When you copy a sentence from the board or write your own, check it against
this short list before you move on:

```example
1. Stor bokstav i början? — A capital letter at the start?
2. Punkt (eller ? eller !) i slutet? — A full stop (or ? or !) at the end?
```
