Your school day at SFI has its own vocabulary, and it repeats every day, so
it is worth knowing well.

```example
en lektion — a lesson
en rast — a break
en läxa — homework
ett schema — a schedule
en lärare — a teacher
```

Put them together in the sentences you actually hear and say at school:

```example
Lektionen börjar klockan nio. — The lesson starts at nine.
Vi har rast klockan tio. — We have a break at ten.
Har du gjort läxan? — Have you done the homework?
Titta på schemat för imorgon. — Look at tomorrow's schedule.
```

Two useful everyday questions ask about time and about what comes next:

```example
När börjar lektionen? — When does the lesson start?
Vad har vi nu? — What do we have now?
```

A few more words describe the people and the room around you, and the test
at the end of a topic:

```example
Läraren heter Maria. — The teacher's name is Maria.
Min klasskamrat kommer från Syrien. — My classmate is from Syria.
Klassrummet är på andra våningen. — The classroom is on the second floor.
Vi har prov på fredag. — We have a test on Friday.
```
