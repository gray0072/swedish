Two small words show where something is: **i** and **på**.

**i** is used for a bigger place — a whole country or a whole city. You are, in a
sense, inside it.

```example
Jag bor i Sverige. — I live in Sweden.
Jag bor i Malmö. — I live in Malmö.
Hon jobbar i Stockholm. — She works in Stockholm.
```

**på** is used for an address — a street and a house number — and for a more exact,
specific spot where you are.

```example
Jag bor på Storgatan 5. — I live at Storgatan 5.
Han jobbar på ett kontor. — He works at an office.
Vi är på skolan. — We are at school.
```

| i | på |
|---|---|
| a country, a city | an address, a specific spot |
| Sverige, Malmö, Stockholm | Storgatan 5, ett kontor, skolan |

Remember: a whole country or a whole city — **i**. A street with a house number, a
building, or a specific spot — **på**.
