In delkurs 2 you learned basic connectors between two sentences (`dessutom`,
`däremot`, `alltså`). An essay is bigger: it needs connectors that mark the
structure of the **whole text** — the opening, developing an argument, contrasting
viewpoints, the conclusion. Like every text connector, they trigger the **V2** rule
when placed at the start of a sentence.

## Connectors by function

| Function | Connectors | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | `inledningsvis`, `till att börja med` | Inledningsvis bör man notera att frågan är omdiskuterad. |
| Adding | `vidare`, `dessutom`, `för det andra` | Vidare visar undersökningen att... |
| Contrasting | `å ena sidan ... å andra sidan`, `däremot` | Å ena sidan sparar man tid; å andra sidan ökar kostnaden. |
| Concluding | `avslutningsvis`, `sammanfattningsvis`, `slutligen` | Avslutningsvis kan man konstatera att båda sidor har goda argument. |

```example
Inledningsvis bör man notera att frågan är starkt omdiskuterad. — To begin with, it's worth noting that the question is hotly debated.
Vidare visar undersökningen att de flesta föredrar distansarbete. — Furthermore, the study shows that most people prefer remote work.
Å ena sidan sparar distansarbete tid, å andra sidan gör det samarbetet svårare. — On the one hand remote work saves time, on the other it makes collaboration harder.
Sammanfattningsvis behövs en balans mellan flexibilitet och gemenskap. — In summary, a balance is needed between flexibility and a sense of community.
```

## An essay skeleton built with these connectors

1. **Inledningsvis** — introduce the topic.
2. **Vidare** / **dessutom** — develop arguments, one after another.
3. **Å ena sidan ... å andra sidan** / **däremot** — the opposing viewpoint.
4. **Avslutningsvis** / **sammanfattningsvis** — the summary, the conclusion.

This is the classic structure of a Swedish argumentative essay (utredande text)
tested in the course's writing exam.

## In short

`Inledningsvis`, `vidare`, `å ena sidan/å andra sidan` and `avslutningsvis` aren't
just isolated words — they mark the structure of the whole essay, so the reader can
immediately see where the introduction is, where the argument develops, where the
contrast comes in, and where the conclusion is. Like any sentence-initial
connector, they require V2: the verb in second position, right after the connector.
