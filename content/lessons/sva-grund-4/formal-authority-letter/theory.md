The most formal register of Swedish is needed when writing to a **myndighet** (public authority) — for instance Migrationsverket, Försäkringskassan or a municipality. Such a letter often opens with a set formula: **Jag vänder mig härmed till [myndighet] angående...** — "I am hereby writing to [authority] regarding...". The word **härmed** ("hereby") and the preposition **angående** ("regarding") set the formal tone right away.

```example
Jag vänder mig härmed till Försäkringskassan angående mitt ärende om föräldrapenning. — I am hereby writing to Försäkringskassan regarding my case about parental benefit.
```

Every approach becomes an **ärende** (a case) with its own **diarienummer** (reference number) — it is worth quoting in correspondence so that the **handläggare** (case officer) can find the right documents quickly.

If you want a formal answer rather than a spoken explanation, you can ask directly: **Jag ber om ett skriftligt beslut i ärendet** — "I am requesting a written decision in the matter." A **beslut** may be a **bifall** (an approval) or an **avslag** (a rejection); a rejection can be **överklaga** (appealed) within a set deadline.

```example
Jag ber om ett skriftligt beslut i ärendet. — I am requesting a written decision in the matter.
Jag vill överklaga avslaget på min ansökan. — I want to appeal the rejection of my application.
```

Sometimes the authority requests a **yttrande** (a statement) from another body. Such a letter also ends formally: **Jag emotser ert svar** ("I look forward to your reply") and the closing **Högaktningsfullt** ("Yours faithfully").
