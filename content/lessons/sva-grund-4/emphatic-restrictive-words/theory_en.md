Written Swedish constantly uses two small families of focusing words: **restrictive**
words (narrow a claim to "exactly this and no more") and **emphatic** words (single
out or intensify one element).

## Restrictive words

| Word | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `endast` | neutral-to-formal, written | Endast medlemmar får delta. |
| `enbart` | neutral-to-formal, written | Kursen är öppen enbart för nybörjare. |
| `bara` | everyday, spoken | Jag vill bara vila lite. |
| `blott` | very formal, literary, rare | Blott ett fåtal klarade provet. |

`Bara` is the same word you already know from spoken Swedish; `endast` and `enbart`
are its neutral-to-formal written equivalents. `Blott` is rare and sounds distinctly
literary — don't overuse it.

```example
Endast medlemmar får delta i mötet. — Only members may take part in the meeting.
Enbart nybörjare kan gå den här kursen. — Only beginners can take this course.
```

## Emphatic words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `just` | precisely, exactly | Det är just den här boken jag menar. |
| `till och med` | even | Till och med barnen förstod det. |
| `själva` | the very, itself/themselves (emphasis) | Själva idén är bra. |

```example
Det är just den här boken jag menar. — It's precisely this book I mean.
Till och med barnen förstod det. — Even the children understood it.
Själva idén är bra, men genomförandet brister. — The idea itself is good, but the execution is lacking.
```

## Position in the sentence

Both restrictive and emphatic words usually sit right before the element they
focus: `endast medlemmar`, `just den här boken`, `till och med barnen`, `själva
idén`. Moving the word changes exactly what falls into focus.

## In short

`Endast`/`enbart` narrow a claim in a neutral-to-formal written style, `bara` is
their spoken equivalent, and `blott` is a rare literary variant. `Just`, `till och
med`, and `själva` single out or intensify one element of the statement. All of them
are placed directly before the word they focus on.
