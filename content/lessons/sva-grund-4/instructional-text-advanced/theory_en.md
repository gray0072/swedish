At delkurs 3 you described a process in general terms, with **inleds med, leder
till**. Now you need to write a full **instruerande text** for a reader who will
actually carry out the steps themselves — for example, filling in an application on
an agency's website. A text like this is not a recipe: it is a guide with clear
steps, anticipated questions, and a check at the end.

## Step markers

```example
Det första steget är att logga in med ditt personnummer. — The first step is to log in with your personal number.
Nästa steg är att fylla i blanketten noggrant. — The next step is to fill in the form carefully.
Det sista steget är att skicka in ansökan digitalt. — The last step is to submit the application digitally.
```

**Det första/nästa/sista steget är att...** — a reliable frame: the reader always
knows where they are in the process.

## Anticipating the reader's questions

A good instructional text answers, in advance, what might go wrong — using a
conditional clause with **om** ("if").

```example
Om du stöter på problemet att sidan inte laddar, prova då att ladda om sidan. — If you encounter the problem that the page doesn't load, then try reloading the page.
Om något inte fungerar som förväntat, kontakta då handläggaren direkt. — If something doesn't work as expected, then contact the caseworker directly.
```

**stöta på (ett problem)** — to encounter (a problem); **fungera som förväntat** —
to work as expected.

## A closing check

An instructional text usually ends with a check before the reader continues or
closes the process.

```example
Kontrollera att alla uppgifter stämmer innan du fortsätter. — Check that all the details are correct before you continue.
Se till att en bekräftelse har kommit innan du stänger sidan. — Make sure a confirmation has arrived before you close the page.
```

## More connectors

- **stöta på** — to encounter, to run into
- **prova att** — to try to (do something)
- **se till att** — to make sure that
- **innan du fortsätter/går vidare** — before you continue/proceed

A text like this combines three parts: the sequence of steps, answers to the
reader's likely problems, and a final check — not just a plain list of actions.
