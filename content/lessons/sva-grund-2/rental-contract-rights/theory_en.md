A tenancy agreement ties together two parties: the **hyresvärd** (landlord)
and the **hyresgäst** (tenant). The **hyresavtal** sets out not just the rent
but each party's duties. The landlord carries an **underhållsskyldighet** — a
duty to keep the property in good condition, including **underhåll**
(ongoing upkeep) and **reparation** (repairs) when things break.

```example
Hyresvärden ansvarar för fastighetens skötsel. — The landlord is responsible for maintaining the property.
Anmäl felet så bokar hyresvärden en reparation. — Report the fault and the landlord will arrange a repair.
```

Most tenants are protected by **besittningsskydd** — the right to keep living
in the apartment even if the landlord wants it back. Rent is set under the
**bruksvärdeshyra** system (use-value rent), and a tenant has the right to a
**skälig hyra** (reasonable rent).

```example
De flesta hyresgäster har starkt besittningsskydd. — Most tenants have strong security of tenure.
Du har rätt att betala en skälig hyra. — You have the right to pay a reasonable rent.
```

An **uppsägning från hyresvärdens sida** (termination by the landlord) usually
requires a valid reason — for example unpaid rent or serious **störningar**
(disturbances). In the worst case this can lead to **förverkande** of the
tenancy and **vräkning** (eviction), though that is always a last resort.

```example
Grannarna anmälde upprepade störningar. — The neighbours reported repeated disturbances.
En vräkning är alltid en sista utväg. — An eviction is always a last resort.
```

Every building usually has its own **ordningsregler** (house rules), and if a
dispute arises between tenant and landlord, help is available from the
**Hyresgästförening** (tenants' association), which also helps negotiate
planned **renoveringar** (renovations) in the building.
