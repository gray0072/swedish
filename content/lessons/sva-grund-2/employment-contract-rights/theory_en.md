When starting a new job in Sweden, you sign an **anställningsavtal** (employment
contract). It states the type of employment: **tillsvidareanställning** (permanent,
literally "until further notice") or **visstidsanställning** (fixed-term, for a set
period). A permanent contract is often preceded by a **provanställning** — a
probationary period, at most six months.

```example
Efter provanställningen fick hon en tillsvidareanställning. — After the probationary period, she got a permanent position.
Han har en visstidsanställning fram till december. — He has a fixed-term contract until December.
```

If an employer wants to **säga upp** (dismiss) an employee, the **uppsägningstid**
(notice period) applies — protected by the **LAS** law (lagen om anställningsskydd, the
Employment Protection Act).

```example
Min uppsägningstid är tre månader. — My notice period is three months.
Enligt LAS har jag rätt till en viss uppsägningstid. — Under LAS, I'm entitled to a certain notice period.
```

Many workplaces are covered by a **kollektivavtal** — a collective agreement that a
**fackförbund** (trade union) negotiates with employers, setting minimum wages and
working conditions. To join a union, members pay a **medlemsavgift** (membership fee)
and become a **medlem** (member).

```example
Jag är med i ett fackförbund. — I am a member of a trade union.
Medlemsavgiften dras från lönen varje månad. — The membership fee is deducted from the salary every month.
```

Unions regularly **förhandlar** (negotiate) over wages, and if talks fail, a **strejk**
(strike) can be the last resort. At the workplace itself, a **skyddsombud** — a safety
representative — is responsible for the working environment.
