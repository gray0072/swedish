Keeping good **kontakt med skolan** (contact with the school) helps you follow your child's everyday school life. A few times a year the school invites parents to a **föräldramöte** (parents' meeting), usually led by the class **lärare** (teacher) or sometimes the **rektor** (headteacher).

```example
Föräldramötet är på tisdag klockan sex. — The parents' meeting is on Tuesday at six.
Jag vill boka en tid med läraren. — I would like to book a meeting with the teacher.
```

Once a **termin** (term), you may also be invited to an individual **utvecklingssamtal** — a short talk with the teacher about how your child, the **elev** (pupil), is doing. This lesson only introduces the word; a fuller version of this conversation is covered later, at SVA grund level. Day to day, most information — **läxor** (homework), **meddelande** (messages), and **betyg** (grades) — is shared through the school's **skolplattform** (online platform), which needs its own **inloggning** (login).

```example
Läxorna finns på skolplattformen. — The homework is on the school platform.
Kontakta mentorn om du har frågor. — Contact the mentor if you have questions.
```

If your child is sick or can't attend, Swedish schools expect a **frånvaroanmälan** (absence report) — you **anmäla frånvaro** (report the absence) yourself, usually online, before the school day starts.

```example
Jag måste anmäla frånvaro för mitt barn idag. — I need to report my child's absence today.
Mitt barn är frånvarande idag på grund av sjukdom. — My child is absent today due to illness.
```

Knowing these phrases makes it much easier to stay in touch with your child's **klass** (class) and **mentor** (homeroom teacher) throughout the school year.
