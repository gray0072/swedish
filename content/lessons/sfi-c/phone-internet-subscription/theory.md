Getting a phone connected in Sweden comes down to a choice: **abonnemang**
(a subscription, billed monthly) or **kontantkort** (a prepaid card, pay as
you go, no contract).

```example
Jag vill teckna ett mobilabonnemang. — I want to sign up for a mobile
subscription.
Jag har kontantkort istället för abonnemang. — I have a prepaid card instead
of a subscription.
```

Whichever you choose, you'll need a **simkort** from an **operatör** (network
provider) — there are several to pick from, and prices vary.

```example
Jag behöver ett nytt simkort. — I need a new SIM card.
Vilken operatör har du? — Which operator do you have?
```

At home, most people set up **bredband** (broadband) for a stable
**uppkoppling**, and connect a phone, computer or **surfplatta** (tablet) to
the **wifi**.

```example
Vi har bredband hemma. — We have broadband at home.
Har ni wifi här? — Do you have wifi here?
Uppkopplingen är långsam idag. — The connection is slow today.
```

Before you sign anything, it's worth asking the price:

```example
Vad kostar abonnemanget per månad? — How much does the subscription cost per
month?
```

And once it's running, keep an eye on the **telefonräkning** (phone bill)
that arrives every month.

```example
Telefonräkningen kom idag. — The phone bill arrived today.
```
