When you **söker jobb** (look for work), you usually start by reading a **jobbannons** (job ad). It lists the **tjänst** (position), whether it's full-time, and what **erfarenhet** (experience) the **arbetsgivare** (employer) wants.

```example
Jag söker jobb som undersköterska. — I'm looking for work as a nursing assistant.
Tjänsten är på heltid. — The position is full-time.
```

If the ad looks right for you, the next step is to **skicka** (send) an **ansökan** (application). Attach your **cv** or **meritförteckning** — both mean roughly the same thing: a written list of your education, jobs, and skills. A polite way to check the process is **"Kan jag skicka min ansökan?"** ("Can I send my application?")

```example
Kan jag skicka min ansökan? — Can I send my application?
Bifoga ditt cv till ansökan. — Attach your CV to the application.
```

If the employer likes what they see, they'll invite you to an **intervju** (interview). They will often ask **"Har du erfarenhet av det här?"** ("Do you have experience with this?") — be ready to answer honestly, and mention a **referens** (reference) from a previous job if you have one.

If it goes well, the company may **anställa** (hire) you. Two useful phrases to have ready are **"Jag söker jobb som ..."** ("I'm looking for work as a ...") to introduce yourself, and **"Jag skulle vilja jobba som ..."** ("I would like to work as a ...") when talking about your goal. With these words, reading an ad and taking the first steps becomes much less intimidating.
