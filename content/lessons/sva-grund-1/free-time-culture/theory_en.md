Talking about a **film** or a **bok** (book) is a common topic in free time.
You usually start by saying what it **handlar om** (is about): **"Jag såg en
film som handlade om..."** ("I saw a film that was about...").

```example
Jag såg en film som handlade om en familj som flyttar till Sverige. — I saw a film about a family that moves to Sweden.
Vilken genre är det — drama eller komedi? — What genre is it — drama or comedy?
```

For a book, you can say where the **handling** (plot) **utspelar sig** (takes
place): **"Boken utspelar sig i..."** ("The book takes place in..."). You'll
often mention the **huvudperson** (main character) and what they experience.

```example
Boken utspelar sig i Stockholm på 1950-talet. — The book takes place in Stockholm in the 1950s.
Huvudpersonen är en ung kvinna som söker jobb. — The main character is a young woman looking for a job.
```

After describing the plot, people usually give their opinion, a bit like a
**recension** (review): **"Jag skulle rekommendera..."** ("I would
recommend...") or **"Det var ganska spännande/tråkigt"** ("It was pretty
exciting/boring").

```example
Jag skulle rekommendera den boken — den var väldigt spännande. — I would recommend that book — it was very exciting.
Filmen var lite tråkig i mitten, tycker jag. — I think the film was a bit boring in the middle.
```

You can borrow books for free at the **bibliotek** (library) or watch films at
the **biograf** (cinema). Discussing what you've read or seen is a simple and
pleasant way to practise Swedish with others.
