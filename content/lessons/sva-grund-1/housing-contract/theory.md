In Sweden, renting a home is split into "first-hand" and "second-hand".
**Förstahandskontrakt** is a first-hand contract: you rent directly from the owner and
are the **förstahandshyresgäst** ("primary tenant"). If the primary tenant temporarily
rents the apartment out to someone else, that's called **andrahandsuthyrning**
("subletting"), and the person renting is the **andrahandshyresgäst** ("subtenant").

```example
Med ett förstahandskontrakt hyr du direkt av fastighetsägaren. — With a first-hand contract you rent directly from the property owner.
Hon hyr lägenheten på ett andrahandskontrakt. — She rents the apartment on a sublet contract.
```

A rental-tenure apartment (**hyresrätt**) is often obtained through the
**bostadskö** ("housing queue") — a queue where you build up waiting time over years. A
co-operative apartment (**bostadsrätt**) works differently: you buy it rather than
renting it.

```example
Han har stått i bostadskön i fem år. — He's been in the housing queue for five years.
```

Terminating a contract — **säga upp kontraktet** — is only possible with an
**uppsägningstid** ("notice period"), usually three months. Before you move out, the
landlord carries out a **besiktning** ("inspection") of the apartment.

```example
Uppsägningstiden är oftast tre månader. — The notice period is usually three months.
Besiktningen sker när du flyttar ut. — The inspection happens when you move out.
```

If a dispute arises between tenant and landlord, you can turn to the
**Hyresnämnden** ("Rental Tribunal"). And when you move out, don't forget the
**flyttstädning** ("move-out cleaning") and getting your **deposition** ("security
deposit") back if the apartment is undamaged.
