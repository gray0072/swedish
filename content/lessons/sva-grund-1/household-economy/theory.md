Planning household finances in Sweden starts with a **budget**, or more precisely a
**hushållsbudget** ("household budget"): a list of **inkomst** ("income") and
**utgift** ("expenses") for the month.

```example
Vi gör en hushållsbudget varje månad. — We make a household budget every month.
Hyran är vår största utgift. — Rent is our biggest expense.
```

Bills — **räkning** — today mostly arrive as an **e-faktura** ("e-invoice") in your
online bank rather than a paper letter. Every bill has a **förfallodag** — the date by
which the money must be paid; if a bill isn't paid on time it **förfaller** ("falls
overdue"), and a **betalningspåminnelse** ("payment reminder") may follow.

```example
Förfallodagen är den sista i månaden. — The due date is the last day of the month.
Jag fick en betalningspåminnelse igår. — I got a payment reminder yesterday.
```

Many people set up **autogiro** — the bank automatically withdrawing money for
recurring bills like rent or insurance, so nothing is forgotten.

```example
Jag betalar hyran via autogiro. — I pay the rent via direct debit.
```

Most people have two accounts: a **lönekonto** ("salary account", where the paycheck
lands) and a **sparkonto** ("savings account", where money is set aside). If money runs
short, people take a **lån** ("loan") — and then it matters to keep an eye on the
**ränta** ("interest rate").

```example
Jag sätter in pengar på sparkontot varje månad. — I put money into my savings account every month.
```
