When retelling someone else's words or text, it's important to distinguish two
different constructions — direct and reported speech — and to know how to reference
a source in a connected text (a referat).

## Direct speech (direkt anföring)

A person's words are passed on verbatim, in quotation marks; the speech verb
(`säger`, `sa`, `frågade`) goes either after the quote with a comma, or before it
with a colon:

```example
”Jag kommer imorgon”, säger Anna. — "I'm coming tomorrow," says Anna.
Anna säger: ”Jag kommer imorgon.” — Anna says: "I'm coming tomorrow."
```

## Reported speech (indirekt anföring)

The quotation marks disappear, the words are rephrased in your own wording, through
an `att`-clause. Pronouns shift to match the third person (`jag` → `hon/han`), and
the word order is the ordinary subordinate-clause order (bisats):

```example
Anna säger att hon kommer imorgon. — Anna says that she's coming tomorrow.
Anna sa att hon inte visste. — Anna said that she didn't know.
```

Compare the whole pair: the same message, two different formats.

```example
”Jag har inte tid”, sa Erik. — "I don't have time," Erik said. (direct speech)
Erik sa att han inte hade tid. — Erik said that he didn't have time. (reported speech)
```

## Referencing a source (källhänvisning)

In a referat — a summary of an article or someone else's text — it's standard
practice to name the source explicitly, rather than presenting the summary as your
own idea:

```example
Enligt en artikel i Dagens Nyheter ökar antalet cyklister i Stockholm. — According to an article in Dagens Nyheter, the number of cyclists in Stockholm is increasing.
Skribenten skriver att situationen har förbättrats. — The writer states that the situation has improved.
```

Typical opening phrases for referencing a source: `Enligt NN...` ("According to
NN..."), `NN skriver att...` ("NN writes that..."), `I artikeln framgår det att...`
("The article shows that..."). Such phrases show the reader exactly where someone
else's idea ends and your own analysis begins — the foundation of an honest,
transparent referat.
