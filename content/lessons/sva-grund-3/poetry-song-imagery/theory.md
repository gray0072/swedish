Poems and songs use language differently from ordinary text: form (stanza,
rhyme) and imagery (metaphor, symbol) both matter. This lesson gives you
words to describe _any_ poem or song — without studying one specific work.

## Form: vers, strof, rim

```example
Dikten har tre strofer med fyra verser i varje. — The poem has three stanzas with four lines each.
```

```example
Sista ordet i varje rad rimmar — det kallas helt enkelt rim. — The last word in each line rhymes — that's simply called rhyme.
```

## Bildspråk: metafor, liknelse, symbol

Bildspråk (figurative language) is when words mean more than the literal:

```example
"Havet är en spegel" är en metafor. — "The sea is a mirror" is a metaphor.
```

```example
"Livet är som en resa" är en liknelse, eftersom man använder ordet "som". — "Life is like a journey" is a simile, because it uses the word "like".
```

```example
En fågel kan vara en symbol för frihet. — A bird can be a symbol of freedom.
```

## Sång: refräng

Songs have one element poems typically don't:

```example
Refrängen upprepas efter varje vers och är ofta det mest minnesvärda. — The refrain repeats after each verse and is often the most memorable part.
```

## How to use this

To describe a poem or song, a few words suffice: how many stanzas (`strof`),
whether it rhymes (`rim`), what imagery is used (`metafor`, `liknelse`,
`symbol`), and whether it has a `refräng`, if it's a song.
