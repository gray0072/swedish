In "referat-summary" you retold a text neutrally, in the third person:
"Artikeln handlar om...", "Författaren menar att...". At this level you go
further and name the source explicitly inside the text — this protects
against plagiarism and makes the referat more credible.

## A full source reference

```example
I en artikel publicerad i Dagens Nyheter den 3 mars skriver Anna Lund att digitaliseringen förändrar arbetsmarknaden. — In an article published in Dagens Nyheter on March 3, Anna Lund writes that digitalization is changing the labour market.
```

## Shorter formulas for repeated references

The full formula is used once at the start; after that, shorter ones work:

```example
Enligt Lund beror förändringen på ny teknik. — According to Lund, the change is due to new technology.
```

```example
Som det står i artikeln, väntas fler jobb försvinna inom handeln. — As it says in the article, more jobs in retail are expected to disappear.
```

## Paraphrasing with attribution

```example
Det framgår av texten att många oroar sig för sina jobb. — It emerges from the text that many are worried about their jobs.
```

## The scheme

1. A full reference at the start: publication + date + author + `skriver att...`.
2. Then shorter references: `enligt [author]`, `som det står i artikeln`.
3. Retell the content in your own words (not a word-for-word quote).
4. No personal opinion — only what the source says.

Just as in the basic referat, there's no "jag tycker" here — only what the
source says — but now it's always clear where the information comes from.
