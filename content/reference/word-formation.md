# Word formation

Swedish builds most of its long words out of short, familiar pieces — once the pieces are
known, a word never seen before is often still guessable.

## Compounds: gluing nouns together

`noun-forms` already showed this for nouns: Swedish joins words directly into one, where
English or Russian would use two or three separate words, usually with a linking **-s-**
between the pieces:

```example
en tandläkare — tand (tooth) + läkare (doctor) = dentist
en arbetslöshetsförsäkring — arbete (work) + lös (-less) + het (-ness) + försäkring (insurance)
```

Compounds are not limited to nouns — adjectives and even whole sentences compress this way:
`klimatsmart` (climate-smart), `sopsortering` (waste sorting).

## Negating prefixes: o- and miss-

- **o-** — the general negating prefix, close to English "un-/in-/non-": `lycklig` (happy) →
  `olycklig` (unhappy); `möjlig` (possible) → `omöjlig` (impossible).
- **miss-** — "wrongly, badly, mis-": `förstå` (understand) → `missförstå` (misunderstand);
  `lyckas` (succeed) → `misslyckas` (fail).

```example
Han är olycklig. — He is unhappy.
Jag tror att du har missförstått. — I think you've misunderstood.
```

`word-formation-o-miss` in the curriculum drills the two prefixes against each other, since
learners often reach for the wrong one (`omissförstå` is not a word).

## Productive suffixes

| Suffix | Turns | into | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-are** | a verb | a person who does it | springa → löpare\* is irregular; regular: måla → målare (painter) |
| **-het** | an adjective | an abstract noun | frisk (healthy) → friskhet (health) |
| **-ning** | a verb | an action noun | undersöka (examine) → undersökning (examination) |
| **-lig** | a noun | an adjective | fara (danger) → farlig (dangerous) |

```example
en målare målar — a painter paints
en undersökning — an examination
```

## Loanwords: absorbed, not translated

Swedish borrows freely from English, especially for technology and work, and immediately
conjugates the loanword as an ordinary group-1 verb (`verb-groups`):

```example
maila — mailar — mailade — mailat (to email)
googla — googlar — googlade — googlat (to google)
```

`framtidssvenska`-style vocabulary — `uppkopplad` (connected), `nedladdning` (download),
`skärm` (screen), `konto` (account) — mixes native compounds with absorbed loans in exactly
this way, which is why guessing from pieces works as often as it does in modern Swedish.
