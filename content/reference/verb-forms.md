# Verb forms

Every form a Swedish verb can take, in one place — the article `verb-groups` gives four of
these (infinitiv, presens, preteritum, supinum); this one shows the rest and how they combine.

## The core forms, for one verb of each group

| Form | tala (1) | ringa (2) | bo (3) | skriva (4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| infinitiv | tala | ringa | bo | skriva |
| presens | talar | ringer | bor | skriver |
| preteritum | talade | ringde | bodde | skrev |
| supinum | talat | ringt | bott | skrivit |
| imperativ | tala! | ring! | bo! | skriv! |

## Compound tenses: perfekt and pluskvamperfekt

Swedish builds these with **har**/**hade** + the supine form — structurally close to English
"have spoken" / "had spoken":

```example
Jag har talat med henne. — I have spoken with her. (perfekt)
Jag hade talat med henne innan du kom. — I had spoken with her before you arrived. (pluskvamperfekt)
```

`tense-system` covers *when* to reach for perfekt instead of preteritum — the single hardest
tense choice for a learner coming from Russian, which does not mark this distinction the same
way.

## Participles

Two participles are built from the verb and used as adjectives or in the passive:

| Participle | Formed from | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **presens particip** | stem + -ande/-ende | en sjungande fågel | a singing bird |
| **perfekt particip** | close to the supine, agrees like an adjective | ett skrivet brev, en skriven bok | a written letter, a written book |

## The passive: three ways

Swedish has three passive constructions, and they are not interchangeable:

- **-s passive** — the everyday, written-register passive, formed by adding **-s** to the
  stem: `Dörren stängs klockan tio` ("The door is closed/gets closed at ten") — states a rule
  or a repeated fact.
- **bli-passive** — `bli` + the perfekt particip — for a single event, something that
  *happens*: `Dörren blev stängd` ("The door was closed", as an event).
- **vara-passive** — `vara` + the perfekt particip — for the resulting *state*:
  `Dörren är stängd` ("The door is closed", describing how things stand right now).

```example
Butiken stängs klockan sex varje dag. — The shop closes at six every day. (rule, -s)
Butiken blev stängd i går. — The shop was closed yesterday. (event, bli)
Butiken är stängd just nu. — The shop is closed right now. (state, vara)
```

See `s-passive-bli-passive` in the curriculum for drills on telling the three apart, and
`modal-verbs` for the bare infinitives that follow `ska`, `kan` and the rest.
