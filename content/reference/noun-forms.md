# Noun forms in full

`noun-genders` covers en/ett and the basic definite suffix; this article lays out every form a
Swedish noun can take, side by side, plus the pieces that article leaves out: irregular
plurals, the genitive, and compounds.

## The four forms, for one noun of each gender

| | en bil (car) | ett hus (house) |
|---|---|---|
| indefinite singular | en bil | ett hus |
| definite singular | bil**en** | hus**et** |
| indefinite plural | bil**ar** | hus |
| definite plural | bil**arna** | hus**en** |

## The five plural patterns, at a glance

| Ending | Typical gender | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -or | en, mostly nouns in -a | en flicka → flickor |
| -ar | en | en bil → bilar |
| -er | en, some ett | en familj → familjer |
| -n | ett, nouns ending in a vowel | ett äpple → äpplen |
| (none) | ett, mostly | ett hus → hus |

## Irregular plurals worth memorising

A short list of very common nouns break the patterns above outright:

```example
en man — män (men, not "manar")
ett barn — barn (children, no plural ending at all)
en fot — fötter (feet, vowel change)
ett öga — ögon (eyes)
en bror — bröder (brothers)
```

## The genitive: -s, no apostrophe

Possession is marked with a bare **-s**, with **no apostrophe** — unlike English:

```example
Annas bok — Anna's book
husets tak — the house's roof / the roof of the house
```

A noun that already ends in **-s**, **-x** or **-z** takes no extra -s: `Lars bil` (Lars's
car), not `Larss bil`. `genitive-longer-phrase` covers the genitive on a whole noun phrase,
where the -s attaches to the *last* word of the phrase, not to the noun it logically belongs to
(`mannen på gatans hund` — "the man on the street's dog").

## Compound nouns: Swedish builds one long word

Where English or Russian would use two or three separate words, Swedish glues them into a
single compound, usually with a linking **-s-**:

```example
en tandläkare — tand (tooth) + läkare (doctor) = dentist
en trafikljus — trafik + ljus = traffic light
en arbetslöshetsförsäkring — arbete + lös + het + försäkring = unemployment insurance
```

The **last** element of a compound always carries the gender and the inflection; everything
before it stays frozen: `en trafikljus` inflects only on `ljus`. `word-formation` covers how to
take a compound apart to guess a word never seen before.
